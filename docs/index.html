Different work. Different assurance.
A clear line between
readiness and certification.
Good automation reduces administration. It does not grant accreditation, erase conflicts of interest or make technical testing a guarantee.
OUR SERVICE SCOPE
Evidence & readiness
Documentation, evidence checks, drift monitoring and scoped technical assessment. Management accepts risk; qualified reviewers own judgment. Agent self-declarations remain labelled as such.
INDEPENDENT DECISION
Certification & attestation
ISO 27001 certifies an organisation's scoped ISMS. ISO 42001 concerns its AI management system. An accredited body makes the certification decision. SOC 2 is an independent CPA attestation, not an ISO certificate.
EXPLICIT AUTHORIZATION
Offensive testing
A separate, written testing scope and rules of engagement. Qualified independent testing, remediation and retest. A vulnerability scan is not a penetration test, and neither certifies your organisation.


The useful questions
Clarity before
the next step.
Can an agent certify itself?
An agent can produce a scoped self-declaration or request a technical assessment. Neither is accredited ISO certification of an organisation. We keep the subject, evidence, issuer, assessment date and limitations explicit so a buyer can tell the difference.
Does continuous mean every control is real-time?
No. Connected technical sources have agreed collection and freshness windows. Human, physical, legal and periodic controls may need interviews, observation or dated sign-off. Delayed, missing and unobservable evidence stays visible; it does not turn green.
Do we have to host our agents with you?
No. The service contract is designed for independently hosted agents and direct organisation teams. Access is scoped and granted by the customer. Finding the service, reading a profile or downloading a brief grants no permission.
How quickly can we become certified?
A scoped evidence snapshot can identify next steps quickly. Genuine certification depends on the management system, operational records, internal audit, management review, corrective actions and the independent body's schedule and decision. Fast document generation does not remove those prerequisites.
Does the service improve by taking our private data?
Our practice improves through independently developed methods, reusable tooling and rights-cleared lessons. Your confidential evidence is not a shared training dataset. Customer records and agreed deliverables remain separate from our background tools, with a portable exit. Cross-customer reuse or model training needs its own explicit rights and permissions; a service engagement is not blanket consent.
What does this preview actually do?
It lets you inspect the service experience: navigation, entry tabs, evidence filtering and selection, contract examples and local downloads. It does not connect systems, submit records, book work, charge money or call a live assessment API. All workspace data is illustrative.
