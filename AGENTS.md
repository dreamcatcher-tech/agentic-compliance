# Repository instructions

## Owning scope

This repository owns only the public Agentic Compliance informational static preview. Its content contract is a complete-feeling, explicitly illustrative service experience: proposed scope and tariffs, fictional evidence, local example downloads and clear assurance boundaries. It does not own a business backend or customer-data system.

Cross-repository working policy is owned by `dreamcatcher-tech/policies`, with its canonical catalogue at `catalog/policies.json`. Consult applicable effective policy through authorized access; do not copy private policy contents or working records here. This public site-only projection does not authorize other public disclosures.

## Publication boundaries

- Keep the public file allowlist to `docs/index.html`, `docs/.nojekyll`, `README.md` and `AGENTS.md` unless separately authorized.
- Never add customer or reviewer data, source conversations, recordings, research, internal economics, credentials, screenshots or private evidence receipts, including in Git history.
- Preserve preview labels, proposed-price wording, illustrative records, null endpoints, explicit consent boundaries and independent-certification distinctions. Do not invent credentials, customers or operating readiness.
- Publish directly from `main:/docs`; retain `.nojekyll`. No custom CI, build pipeline, accounts, forms, payments, analytics or external APIs are needed.
- Before publication, inspect complete changed bytes and history, perform bounded privacy checks, and verify exact remote files and live HTTPS behavior. Never force-push or overwrite another owner's work.
